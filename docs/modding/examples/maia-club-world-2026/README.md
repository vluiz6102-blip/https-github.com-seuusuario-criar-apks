# Maia Soccer Manager: Club World 2026/27

## Coverage

**152 clubs, 8 competitions and 3,344 fictional player records.** Each club has 22 players: 3 goalkeepers, 7 defenders, 7 midfielders and 5 forwards.

Included competitions:
- Premier League 2026/27: 20 clubs
- La Liga 2026/27: 20 clubs
- Bundesliga 2026/27: 18 clubs
- Serie A 2026/27: 20 clubs
- Ligue 1 2026/27: 18 clubs
- Brasileirão Série A 2026: 20 clubs
- Primeira Liga 2026/27: 18 clubs
- Eredivisie 2026/27: 18 clubs

## Original fictional player data

All 3,344 players in this pack are generated specifically for Maia Soccer Manager. Names, ages, positions, ratings, potential and market values are fictional game data, not official player identities or a verified real-world roster. Player nationalities follow their club's home-country code.

No real-player database, player portraits, official crests or official kits are included. Club and competition names are reference labels; the game uses its own logo, visual identity and player renderer.

## Generation and license

The deterministic generation logic lives in `scripts/generate-original-player-data.mjs`. The generated player records are released under **CC0 1.0** for reuse without attribution requirements. The main game's source-code license is separate and remains documented at the repository root.
